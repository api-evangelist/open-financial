# Open — AI-Native Corporate Internet Banking

> Open is an AI-native corporate internet banking OS for the CFO office — a software layer that connects to 40+ Indian banks, unifying cash visibility, payments, payables, receivables, reconciliation, treasury, compliance, and spend management in one platform. Open is not a bank; it is the operating system running on top of your existing banking relationships.

Key terms: corporate internet banking, neobanking, CFO office, multi-bank connectivity, payment aggregator, accounts payable, accounts receivable, treasury management, reconciliation, escrow, spend management, GST compliance, RBI, India fintech, B2B payments, AI agents, agentic finance

## Platform Overview

- [Home](https://open.money/): AI-native corporate internet banking for India's finance teams — 10 product surfaces, one operating system for the CFO office.
- [AI Agents](https://open.money/agents): Supervised AI agents that handle categorisation, reconciliation, forecasting, vendor payments, and collections. Humans stay in control; agents handle the volume.
- [Pricing](https://open.money/pricing): Custom pricing across three named tiers — Essential (growing finance teams), Growth (high-volume automated workflows), Enterprise (multi-entity, custom integrations, dedicated success). No fixed public tier pricing; quote-based on banking setup, transaction volumes, workflows, integrations, automation needs, and support requirements.

## Sibling Platforms

Open Financial Technologies operates three distinct platforms:

- **Open** (this site, https://open.money): Corporate internet banking OS — the CFO office platform.
- **Zwitch** (https://zwitch.io): India's leading payment gateway for developers and platforms.
- **BankingStack** (https://bankingstack.com): AI-native digital banking OS for banks themselves. Three of India's top five banks run their corporate internet banking on BankingStack.

Combined across all three: 4 million+ businesses served, $300 billion+ in online payments processed.

## Core Products

Open has ten product surfaces that share a common data layer across all your bank accounts and entities.

### Cash & Visibility

- [Multi-Bank Connectivity](https://open.money/product/multi-bank): Connect every bank account and entity across 40+ Indian banks in one dashboard. Real-time balances, statements, and payments without logging into 10 separate portals.
- [Smart Statement](https://open.money/product/smart-statement): AI parses, classifies, and reconciles bank statements from 40+ Indian banks. Every transaction is tagged at source — category, vendor, cost centre — so your books are always clean.
- [Treasury](https://open.money/product/treasury): Live 13-week rolling cash forecast, automated sweeps, FD laddering, and FX management. Know your runway to the day.

### Payments & Workflows

- [Payments](https://open.money/product/payments): All payment rails in one workflow — NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, and international wires. Maker-checker approvals, bulk payouts, and real-time routing via lowest cost.
- [Payables](https://open.money/product/payables): End-to-end AP automation — vendor onboarding, invoice OCR, three-way match, maker-checker, bulk payouts. Purchase-to-pay cycle reduced from 14 days to 3.2 days on average.
- [Receivables](https://open.money/product/receivables): AR automation — invoicing, payment links, auto-reconciliation. DSO reduced by 17 days on average across Open customers.

### Intelligence & Control

- [Smart Ledger](https://open.money/product/smart-ledger): AI-native ledger with three-way reconciliation across payment gateway, bank, and marketplace. Query your books in plain English.
- [Spend Management](https://open.money/product/spend-management): Agentic spend control on corporate cards — issue cards, set policies, capture receipts, real-time reconciliation. Finance teams approve; agents process.
- [Compliance](https://open.money/product/compliance): GST filing inside the banking screen — GSTR-1, 3B, 9, ITC reconciliation, and TDS automation.
- [Escrow & Cash Management](https://open.money/product/escrow): RBI-licensed escrow with bank-level fund segregation. Virtual accounts and enterprise-scale CMS for marketplaces, real estate, and regulated fund flows.
